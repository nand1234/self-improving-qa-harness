# REF-101 — Refund for a damaged item

**As a** customer
**I want to** report an item that arrived damaged
**So that** I get a refund without the hassle of sending it back

## Acceptance criteria
1. Within 30 days of delivery, a customer can report an item as damaged and upload between 1 and 5 photos (JPG or PNG, max 10 MB each).
2. A damaged-item refund is issued without the customer returning the item.
3. Each item can be reported as damaged only once.
4. Support staff can see the photos attached to the claim.

## Example
Order #60455, ceramic vase, €75.00.
