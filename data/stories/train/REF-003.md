# REF-003 — Manager approval for high-value refunds

**As a** finance manager
**I want** large refunds to need my approval
**So that** we reduce the risk of fraud and mistakes

## Acceptance criteria
1. Refund requests above €500.00 are set to **Pending Approval** instead of being processed immediately.
2. A user with the **Manager** role can approve or reject a pending refund. A reason is required when rejecting.
3. Approved refunds are processed; rejected refunds are closed.
4. The customer is notified by email of the outcome.

## Example
Order #30877, laptop, €1,249.00.
