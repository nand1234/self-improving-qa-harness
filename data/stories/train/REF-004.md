# REF-004 — Refunds to the original payment method

**As a** customer
**I want** my refund to go back the way I paid
**So that** I don't have to chase my money

## Acceptance criteria
1. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.
2. If the order was paid partly with a gift card and partly by card, the gift card portion is returned as a new gift card and the rest goes back to the card.
3. The refund confirmation shows how much was returned to each payment method.

## Example
Order #40102, total €150.00, paid with €50.00 gift card + €100.00 Mastercard.
